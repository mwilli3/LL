# PRODUCT.md — The Regulated Child (TRC)

register: brand

## Product purpose
A suite of free, interactive "lead magnet" web apps for **The Regulated Child** (brand: *Larice*, social: **@regulatedchild**), a parent-education brand teaching nervous-system science for child behavior. Each app is gated behind a name/email capture and ends by routing the parent toward the paid course and other free tools. They are consumed almost entirely on phones, mostly from TikTok.

The five apps:
- **Regulation Profile Quiz** — 8-question quiz returning one of three child "regulation profiles" (Sensory Seeker, Sensitive Regulator, Slow-to-Recover). *Template app.*
- **In-the-Moment Scripts Pack** — searchable/emergency script library.
- **Co-Regulation Guide** — 5-step protocol walkthrough.
- **Behavior Decoder** — behavior → nervous-system-state reference.
- **The Body Behind the Behavior** — 5-section science explainer.

## Users
Stressed, caring parents (overwhelmingly mothers) of dysregulated children, reading on a phone, often mid- or post-meltdown. They are not clinicians. They want to feel understood, not judged, and to get something they can use immediately.

## Brand voice (physical-object words)
Warm. Grounded. Clinically careful but tender. Like a trusted, well-read pediatric therapist who speaks plainly. Earthy, not pastel-wellness; credible, not cold-medical.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS quiz (centered white card, drop shadow, progress pills).
- Pastel "self-care" wellness aesthetics.
- Cold/clinical medical UI.
- The AI "editorial" cliché (thin italic Fraunces/Playfair display + tiny tracked mono labels everywhere).

## Strategic principles
- Calm is functional, not decorative: the parent is often dysregulated themselves.
- Every screen should feel authored and credible (research-cited) without feeling like homework.
- The exit always points to: comment keywords on @regulatedchild, and regulatedchild.com.
- Self-contained single-file React artifacts (inline styles, `useState`, optional `window.storage`, fonts via `<link>`). No build step, no external CSS/Tailwind.
